Campaign Overview

The National Service Scheme (NSS) Unit of Government Higher Secondary School, Vettathur actively participated in an Anti-Drug Awareness Campaign organized in collaboration with the Excise Department on 5 June 2026. The program was conducted with the objective of educating students and the public about the harmful effects of drug abuse and promoting a healthy, responsible, and drug-free lifestyle.

More than 70 participants joined the campaign, including NSS volunteers, Junior Red Cross (JRC) members, teachers, senior educators, school administrators, Excise officials, and community representatives. The event demonstrated the united commitment of educational institutions and government agencies in combating substance abuse among youth.

Event Details

  • Date: 05 June 2026, Friday
  • Time: 10:00 AM
  • Venue: Vettathur Town & Nearby Public Areas
  • Organized By: Excise Department & NSS Unit, GHSS Vettathur

Awareness Topics Covered

  • Drug addiction and its consequences
  • Illegal sale & distribution of narcotics
  • Social & family impact of substance abuse
  • Youth safety & responsible decision-making
  • Community role in preventing drug abuse

Awareness Rally Through the Community

The program began with a large awareness rally led by officials from the Excise Department. Participants marched through important public areas carrying banners and messages promoting a drug-free society. The rally attracted the attention of local residents and encouraged meaningful discussions about the dangers of addiction and substance abuse.

Educational Sessions and Cultural Programs

Following the rally, participants gathered for an awareness session where Excise Department officials delivered informative talks on the growing challenges posed by substance abuse. Officers explained the legal consequences of drug-related activities and highlighted the importance of early intervention, awareness, and community vigilance.

Students contributed to the campaign through cultural programs designed to spread anti-drug messages in creative and engaging ways. Performances included:

  • Awareness-themed dance programs
  • Poetry presentations on the dangers of addiction
  • Motivational messages promoting healthy lifestyles
  • Student-led awareness activities against substance abuse

These performances effectively communicated the dangers of addiction while encouraging positive life choices among young people.

Messages from School Leadership

The Headmaster and senior teachers addressed the gathering and emphasized the crucial role of students in building a healthy society. They encouraged participants to remain vigilant, avoid harmful influences, and become ambassadors of awareness within their families and communities.

Speakers highlighted that preventing substance abuse is not only the responsibility of law enforcement agencies but also a collective duty shared by schools, parents, students, and society as a whole.

Refreshments and Community Spirit

After the program, all participants were provided with tea and snacks by the school management as a gesture of appreciation for their active involvement. The gathering also provided an opportunity for students, teachers, and officials to interact and exchange ideas about future awareness initiatives.

Impact and Outcome

The Anti-Drug Campaign 2026 successfully raised awareness among students and community members regarding the dangers of substance abuse. The event strengthened cooperation between the school, NSS volunteers, and the Excise Department while encouraging young people to make informed and responsible choices.

Through awareness, education, and community participation, the program contributed to the broader mission of creating a healthier, safer, and drug-free future for society.

Anti-Drug Pledge

One of the most significant moments of the event was the Anti-Drug Pledge administered by the Excise Officer. Students, teachers, and volunteers collectively pledged to:

  • Stay away from drugs and all addictive substances, and encourage peers to do the same.

  • Promote healthy habits, responsible behaviour, and positive life choices in daily life.

  • Support future awareness initiatives and become ambassadors of change within the community.

  • Encourage friends and family to lead drug-free lives and contribute to a safer, healthier society.

The pledge was administered by the Excise Officer and taken collectively by all 70+ participants of the campaign.
