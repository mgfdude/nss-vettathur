NSS Unit, Vettathur
Special Camp Case Study

SAPTHADIN CAMP 2025–26

"Seven Days of Service, Leadership and Community Living"

GLP School, Thelakkad
26 Dec 2025 – 01 Jan 2026
52 Volunteers
7 Days
350+ Service Hours
800+ Beneficiaries

The National Service Scheme (NSS) Unit No. NSS/SFU/MPM/HSE/81 of Government Higher Secondary School, Vettathur successfully organized its Seven-Day Special Camp, "SAPTHADIN CAMP 2025–26", at GLP School, Thelakkad from 26 December 2025 to 1 January 2026.

Conducted under the theme "Youth for the Integrity of the Village – The Lifeblood of Human Compassion", the camp brought together fifty dedicated NSS volunteers for a week of community service, leadership development, environmental awareness, and social responsibility.

Throughout the camp, volunteers actively engaged in a variety of meaningful activities aimed at improving the local community and developing their own sense of civic responsibility. Major initiatives included cleanliness drives, environmental conservation programs, awareness campaigns, community surveys, leadership training sessions, group discussions, cultural activities, and public interaction programs.

Key Initiatives

  • Construction of organic waste pits
  • School beautification and classroom renovation
  • Local well-water survey
  • Leadership forums and awareness programs

Camp Outcomes

  • Improved public spaces
  • Increased community engagement
  • Environmental awareness
  • Leadership development & teamwork

One of the highlights of the camp was the volunteers' contribution to local development through community-centered projects. Volunteers worked together to improve public spaces, support environmental sustainability efforts, and strengthen relationships between the school, NSS, and the local community. The camp also provided opportunities for participants to learn teamwork, discipline, self-reliance, and leadership through shared living and collective responsibility.

In addition to service activities, the camp featured educational sessions, cultural evenings, sports, and interactive programs that encouraged creativity, cooperation, and social awareness. Volunteers also took part in campaigns promoting healthy lifestyles, environmental protection, social harmony, and responsible citizenship.

The successful completion of SAPTHADIN CAMP 2025–26 stands as a testament to the dedication of NSS volunteers, the guidance of program officers, the support of parents, and the cooperation of the local community. The experience left a lasting impact on both the participants and the residents, reinforcing the NSS mission of building socially responsible and compassionate citizens.

"The seven days at GLP School, Thelakkad were not merely a camp—they were a journey of service, friendship, leadership, and community transformation."

Not Me But You

This philosophy was reflected throughout the seven days as volunteers lived together, prepared for activities, participated in awareness campaigns, and dedicated their time and energy to serving society. Mobile phones and unnecessary distractions were intentionally avoided, allowing participants to focus on community engagement, personal growth, and meaningful interactions.
