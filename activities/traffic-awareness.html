Campaign Overview

The NSS Unit of GHSS Vettathur organized an essential Census 2027 Self Enumeration Orientation Class on 12 June 2026. The session aimed to educate volunteers about the upcoming national census, digital self-enumeration methods, public awareness strategies, and effective community engagement.

The programme focused on road safety rules, responsible driving behavior, helmet awareness, seat belt usage, pedestrian safety, traffic signs and signals, and accident prevention. The session encouraged students to become responsible road users and safety ambassadors within their communities.

Event Overview

Understanding Traffic Safety: Traffic awareness is crucial in preventing accidents and saving lives. The session provided deep insights into why every citizen must prioritize road safety.

The session, led by Road Safety Experts Melattur Highway Police Team, aimed to educate volunteers about digital self-enumeration methods, public awareness strategies, and effective community engagement to ensure no citizen is left uncounted.

Learning Traffic Rules

The officers discussed vital regulations, signals, and signs that govern our roads. They also highlighted safety equipment awareness, stressing the life-saving importance of helmets, seat belts, reflectors, and visibility during night travel.

Anti-Drug Pledge

One of the most significant moments of the event was the Anti-Drug Pledge administered by the Excise Officer. Students, teachers, and volunteers collectively pledged to:

  • Stay away from drugs and all addictive substances, and encourage peers to do the same.

  • Promote healthy habits, responsible behaviour, and positive life choices in daily life.

  • Support future awareness initiatives and become ambassadors of change within the community.

  • Encourage friends and family to lead drug-free lives and contribute to a safer, healthier society.

The pledge was administered by the Excise Officer and taken collectively by all 70+ participants of the campaign.
